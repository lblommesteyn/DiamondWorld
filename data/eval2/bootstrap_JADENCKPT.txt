## Why the paired CI is the right one

For reference, the analytic (Fisher-z) SE on a SINGLE correlation at n=382 is about +/-0.043 in r units around the baseline AVG. That is the width of the unpaired view above and it is far too wide to resolve the differences this project cares about. The paired CI is narrower because both models see the same batters, so shared batter-level noise cancels in the difference.

- v22pf_s42_committed AVG: unpaired CI width 0.070, paired-vs-J_nohist CI width 0.090 (0.8x tighter).

- J_hist AVG: unpaired CI width 0.088, paired-vs-J_nohist CI width 0.071 (1.2x tighter).
