Reading it. A positive partial correlation of the expected-hit feature with the
realized hit rate, AFTER conditioning on the model's own prediction, means the
model is not extracting information it was handed. That is a fixable modelling
defect, not a data ceiling, and it is the opposite of the 'we are at the
information limit of the feature set' reading in RESULTS.md.

The low-PA / high-PA split is the mechanism test: columns 5:7 are filled AFTER
per-stat shrinkage runs on columns 0..3 and are never shrunk, so if the feature
is much weaker on the low-PA half then it is arriving unregularised and the fix
is to shrink it with the same 2200-PA constant the raw hit column already gets.
