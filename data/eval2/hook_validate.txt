BULLPEN HOOK MODEL: state-dependent starter-pull hazard

  features: pas, tto, ra  (inning dropped: collinear with PAs faced)
  base pull rate per starter-PA: 0.0433
  standardized logistic coefficients:
    intercept -4.790
    pas       +1.938
    tto       +0.022
    ra        +0.292
  (pas + and ra + are the right signs: more batters faced and more runs
   allowed each raise the pull probability.)

  held-out 2023: log-loss 0.1326 vs constant base-rate 0.1845 (28% better); AUC 0.889; n=106,478

  managerial behaviour (held-out 2023): pull rate by runs allowed at a
  fixed workload of 18-21 PAs faced (the 6th-7th-inning decision zone):
    runs allowed    real pull    model       n
    0-1                 0.061    0.047    7189
    2-3                 0.067    0.069    5680
    4+                  0.108    0.109    2915
