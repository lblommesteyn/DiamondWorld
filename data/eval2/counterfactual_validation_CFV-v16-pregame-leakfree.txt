COUNTERFACTUAL VALIDATION vs market repricing (CFV-v16-pregame-leakfree) -- 2355 games with odds

1. TEAM FIXED-EFFECTS residual (controls team strength + home field, no outcomes):
   corr(sim, market) game-specific = 0.248

2. WITHIN-SERIES (home field constant -> starter/park/rest only): 646 series, 2343 game-deviations
   corr(sim dWP, market dWP)      = 0.237   (permutation null 95th pct 0.035; SIGNIFICANT)
   OLS slope (market ~ sim)       = 0.163
   noise-corrected slope          = 0.483   (sim replica-noise reliability 0.34; 1.0 = magnitudes match market)

3. VERDICT
   Direction: the simulator's causal starter effects agree with the market
   (leak-free, significant). Magnitude: the raw simulator overstates effects;
   multiply raw counterfactual WP deltas by ~0.48 to put them in
   market-calibrated units. Example: a raw +9.8-point ace swap becomes ~+4.7 points calibrated.
   (The remaining gap to slope 1.0 is real over-reaction plus residual market
   noise; more replicas would sharpen the estimate.)
