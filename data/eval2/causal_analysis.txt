DESIGN-BASED CAUSAL ANALYSIS of the within-series validation

  2343 within-series game-deviations, 646 series

  (1) rest-day control (dependent: market within-series dWP):
      simulator dWP coef, no control:   0.267  (t 16.4)
      simulator dWP coef, + rest diff:   0.266  (t 16.3)   rest coef 0.0110 (t 3.2)
      -> the simulator effect is essentially unchanged by controlling for rest.

  (2) negative-control / placebo (market deltas permuted across series):
      placebo corr: mean +0.000, 95th pct 0.035, 99th 0.048
      observed corr 0.321 is far outside the placebo distribution.

  (3) sensitivity to unobserved confounding:
      to fully explain the association, an omitted differenced covariate would need
      correlation >= 0.57 with BOTH the simulator delta and the market delta
      (e.g. 0.57x0.57 = 0.32 = r). Rest, the strongest observed
      differenced covariate, correlates +0.07 with the market
      delta, well below that bar, so no single plausible confounder explains the effect.

  Honest scope: this is a first-difference design with stated assumptions, not a
  timestamp-clean scratch event study (the announced-probable data is not public).
