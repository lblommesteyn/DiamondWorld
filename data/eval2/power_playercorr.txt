# Power analysis of the player-corr gate

382 batters with >= 150 PA, 2000 simulated seasons x 4000 bootstrap reps, 95% CI, incumbent data/eval2/prod_rates_v16.npz

## 1. The attenuation ceiling

| stat | var(true) | binomial noise | reliability | max attainable corr | incumbent corr | headroom |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K | 0.00312 | 0.00050 | 0.862 | 0.929 | 0.792 | +0.136 |
| BB | 0.00062 | 0.00023 | 0.727 | 0.852 | 0.651 | +0.201 |
| Hit | 0.00043 | 0.00047 | 0.477 | 0.691 | 0.445 | +0.246 |
| HR | 0.00013 | 0.00008 | 0.630 | 0.794 | 0.610 | +0.184 |
| **AVG** | | | | **0.816** | **0.624** | **+0.192** |

Reliability is the share of the observed spread in batter rates that is real skill rather than binomial luck; its square root is the correlation a model that knew every true rate exactly would score.

Error correlation between the two supplied models, used to pair the simulated pair: K 0.693, BB 0.785, Hit 0.757, HR 0.821 (mean 0.764).

## 2. Power of the gate against a true AVG improvement

| true AVG delta | P(CI excludes zero) |
|---|---|
| +0.000 | 0.04 |
| +0.010 | 0.17 |
| +0.015 | 0.31 |
| +0.020 | 0.49 |
| +0.025 | 0.71 |
| +0.030 | 0.85 |
| +0.035 | 0.95 |
| +0.040 | 0.98 |
| +0.050 | 1.00 |

Minimum detectable effect at 80% power: **+0.030 AVG**.

False-positive rate at delta = 0 is 0.044, which should sit near 0.05 if the gate is correctly calibrated.
