   OLS slope (market ~ sim)       = 0.218
   noise-corrected slope          = 0.430   (sim replica-noise reliability 0.51; 1.0 = magnitudes match market)

3. VERDICT
   Direction: the simulator's causal starter effects agree with the market
   (leak-free, significant). Magnitude: the raw simulator overstates effects;
   multiply raw counterfactual WP deltas by ~0.43 to put them in
   market-calibrated units. Example: a raw +9.8-point ace swap becomes ~+4.2 points calibrated.
   (The remaining gap to slope 1.0 is real over-reaction plus residual market
   noise; more replicas would sharpen the estimate.)
